Storyboard: the Budget 2027 solar battery subsidy, as a white model

One continuous crane over a single white site board: Parliament on its island, the street with the hero house and its battery rack, a neighbourhood of homes behind it, and the port at the far end. The orange accent always means the subsidy; it leaves the budget book and travels the model along thin conduits. The timings come from the 20-line subtitle file (0:00 to 1:37); the piece runs 98 s.

Facts and numbers I had to assume

  • Timing comes from the subtitle file only (as you asked). Word times inside each line are estimated from where the word sits in its line. The attached voice.wav runs 1:43, about 6 s longer than the subtitles, so if it is muxed in it will drift. Tell me whether the final MP4 should carry the voice or be picture + effects only.
  • Subsidy maths, as in the script: Rs 25,000 per kWh of battery. 5 kWh = Rs 125,000, 8 kWh = Rs 200,000, 10 kWh or more = Rs 250,000 (the cap per home). The readout counts these exactly.
  • Rs 3.5 billion fund: shown as a label. The neighbourhood holds 12 model homes. That's not a literal count: at the cap the fund covers about 14,000 homes (3.5 bn ÷ 250,000). I left that derived number off screen.
  • The levy is labelled "10% LEVY · port & airport development" (the Port and Airport Development Levy, PAL). The saving is shown as Rs 4,000–5,000 per kWh, as stated in the script.
  • Net Plus is shown as the house meter and its line to the grid, labelled "new solar + battery only".
  • No people are depicted. The President is mentioned in the voice only; on screen, the beat shows the Parliament model and a PARLIAMENT label. The Parliament is drawn as a simple cluster of pavilions with stacked hipped roofs on an island, a nod to the real building, not a replica.
  • On-screen words are English (Inter / IBM Plex Mono, as specified). These fonts have no Sinhala glyphs. If you want Sinhala labels, I'll add Noto Sans Sinhala.
  • Stills are block-outs at 16 samples, rendered in software (no GPU here), so depth of field and shadows are slightly grainier than in the final.
